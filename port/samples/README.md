# Sample files for Phase 13a

Two small files to try the import on the phone without touching your real
schedule. Every class and assignment in them ends in "(sample)", so they're
easy to find and delete afterwards.

- `classping-sample.ics`: a calendar. Two weekly classes (Biology Mon/Wed/Fri
  9:00, Statistics Tue/Thu 13:30, both until Dec 18 2026), three deadlines
  (an essay due Nov 3 at 23:59, a midterm on Nov 12 at 10:00, a final project
  on Dec 15, all day), a quiz in the past and a cancelled seminar (both
  skipped).
- `classping-sample.csv`: a schedule spreadsheet. Three classes (Chemistry,
  History, Studio Art) and the semester's days, Aug 31 to Dec 18 2026.

To get them onto the phone: iCloud Drive for Windows, or email them to
yourself and use Save to Files.
