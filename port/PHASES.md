# ClassPing iOS: the phases

*Step 2 deliverable from [IOS_APP.md](../../IOS_APP.md), planned from [WEB-TO-IOS-MAP.md](WEB-TO-IOS-MAP.md) and Kelvin's answers of 2026-10-13 ([DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md)).*

**Shape of the plan:** models and logic first, then screens, then notifications, then sign-in and sync last. Every phase ends with something to tap on the phone, adds at most three new Swift concepts, and touches 3 to 8 files (Phase 4 was split into 4a and 4b, Phase 5 into 5a and 5b, Phase 6 into 6a and 6b, Phase 7 into 7a, 7b and 7c, Phase 8 into 8a, 8b, 8c and 8d, Phase 11 into 11a and 11b+c, and Phase 13 into 13a and 13b, to stay inside that: D-17, D-20, D-24, D-29, D-36, D-40, D-70, D-72, D-79; 11b+c is over the rule at Kelvin's request, typed in three stops). No deadlines: a phase is done when its checklist is, not when a date passes. Phase 12 is on hold while Kelvin reworks the gamification, so 13 comes first (D-79).

## What the free Apple ID blocks (not coming)

- Push notifications from the server (needs APNs, a paid account). Local notifications cover reminders while the app is installed.
- Home-screen widgets that read the app's data, and universal links (App Groups and Associated Domains are paid features).
- Installing on anyone else's phone, and signing that lasts longer than 7 days.

## "Fully coded" means

Every Port, Rebuild, Connect and Replace row in the map is built and checked on the phone (except the boss fight); every ported logic file passes Swift versions of the web tests; the five test planners in `docs/PRODUCT_RULES.md` behave; and the only things missing are the ones listed just above.
